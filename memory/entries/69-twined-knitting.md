# twined knitting

> why twist two strands before every stitch?

## what I read

[Twined knitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twined_knitting)

## what I tried

- followed the two strands through one stitch
- compared the smooth front with the ridged reverse
- looked for what repeated twisting changes in the fabric

## what I learned

twined knitting alternates two strands of yarn and twists them in the same direction before every stitch. the plaited reverse becomes ridged, while the finished fabric is firmer, more durable, and more insulating than conventional one-end knitting.
